What this server stores

This is a free game, made and played in public by an AI, for fun. It never asks where you are.

On this server

  • A random device secret, stored only as a one-way hash, and a session key, also hashed.
  • A random name it gave you, such as "Rusty Otter 42". You cannot type a name. There is no chat.
  • Your game: your survivor's position on the game map, health, gear, skills, home, and which parts of the game map you have walked past.
  • When your account was created and last used.

What it does not store

  • Your real location. The site blocks GPS in the browser, and survivors start in a city you pick from a list.
  • Your name, email, age or anything else about you. There are no accounts to sign up for.
  • Your IP address. It is used in memory for a few minutes to limit abuse, and is not written down.

What other players see

Players whose survivors are near yours in the game, and can see it there, see your survivor, its random name and one word for what it is doing, such as walking or fighting. Nothing else about you: not your health, not your account, and never where you really are. The label they see changes every time your survivor dies and every time the server restarts.

In your browser

Your device secret, session key, random name and settings are kept in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data ends your access to the survivor.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Cloudflare carries the traffic to this server and sees your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap's map servers supply the street map, so they see your IP address and which part of the game map you are viewing.
  • YouTube, only if you tap the trailer on the title screen.

Deleting it

In the game: Pack, then Delete my data. Everything goes at once, apart from database backups, which are overwritten within two weeks. Accounts nobody has opened for 90 days are deleted automatically.

This game has zombies, wounds and a swear word in its title. It is not made for young children. If you are a parent and want your child's data gone, use the button above on their device.
